30-second decision brief
1 · Binding constraint

Staffing is the binding constraint at peak; 28 servers are running at 95.8% capacity utilization.

2 · One move · C

Add 10 servers, increasing staffing from 28 to 38, to create capacity during peak periods.

3 · Proof

Completed cups rise from 5,100 to 6,300 (+1,200, about +23.5%). Cost to serve rises from $1.74 to $1.91 per cup (+$0.17).

4 · Win–win?

Customers get much shorter waits (about 15 to 4 minutes). The company is projected to gain $15,000+ in revenue and $4,000+ in net income, although cost per cup increases.

5 · Next constraint

Equipment capacity becomes the next ceiling to monitor; the source analysis says the current equipment can handle about 6,300 cups/week, but should be monitored as volume grows.
